Parser

Day-first tenants send dates day first. The shared parser reads the month first, so 7 April lands on 4 July. This is the app, email, and booking-v2 reports. The desk's table keeps the one-day reports inside this count.

Hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People type the month first, as the hint says. The form then reads the day first, the tenant's way. Same swapped day, different code.

One day

A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. One invite arrives before the booking was made.
